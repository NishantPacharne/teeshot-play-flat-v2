Fair play

Every shot is filmed on the course camera and measured by TeeShot AI from where the ball comes to rest to the cup. Shots flagged for review are checked by staff before they count. Daily winners are announced at the end of the day, 11:30 PM Toronto time; ties go to the earlier shot. Players must be 16+.
